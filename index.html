Last call: the week that was
As told by the guy at the end of the bar

Pull up a stool. This whole league was Will's idea, he set the rules, let the draft order randomize itself an hour before, and drew pick six like a man who trusts the process. Eight teams, full PPR, and by the time the board settled, the guy who built the thing landed dead in the middle of it. No notes, as advertised.

Oscar let the machine do the picking, full autodraft, start to finish, and the machine landed him second in the league. Chase, Lamb, Bowers, Jeanty, Hall. Eighteen rounds of a computer clicking best-player-available, and it still remembered every position except defense. There's a lesson in there somewhere and nobody's looking for it.

Robert mostly let it ride too, but he phoned in for a few picks, and those were the two most talked-about selections of the entire draft: a Texans D/ST in round six, and Harrison Butker in round seven. Nobody's explained either one. Nobody's asked twice.

Nic had the very first pick in the draft and finished it in fourth place, which is its own kind of achievement. Bijan at the top is real, but he's also carrying three tight ends into a league that starts one, and he had two of them before Tuesday and decided that was not enough.

Speaking of Tuesday, two trades, same night, wildly different outcomes. Tom sent Kenneth Walker III and Tee Higgins to Robert for Amon-Ra St. Brown, Robert cut Brock Purdy to make the deal fit, and both lineups came out looking better for it. The same night, Skoney sent out Chris Olave, Brian Thomas Jr. and his only tight end, Colston Loveland, to Nic for A.J. Brown and Jaylen Waddle. Nic cut Deebo Samuel to make room, picked up two more tight ends than he needed, and Skoney's tight end room is now a rumor.

Which brings us to the empty chairs around here. Oscar and Brennan both went the full eighteen rounds without drafting a defense, bold from the two best week 1 lineups behind Tom. And Skoney's TE slot has been sitting open since Tuesday night, unfilled, unbothered, doing its best impression of furniture.

Kickoff is tonight. Patriots at Seahawks, 7:20 Central, the only game on the board, and it just so happens to touch Tom's matchup, Stevenson, Price and the Seahawks D/ST all take the field before Skoney's roster has anyone in the building. Grab a pint, the season starts now.
