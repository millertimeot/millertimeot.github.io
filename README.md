# Miller Time Chalkboard

This is the site itself. Two parts of it now update live, straight from a shared
backend, so the league doesn't have to wait for someone to edit the page.

## The napkin wall

Say whatever you want to the whole bar. Type your name and a note (240
characters, same as everywhere else on the site), hit "Pin it," and it shows
up at the top of the wall for everyone, no page reload needed. Notes look
like napkins, newest first, and refresh automatically every minute while
you've got the tab open.

## Side bets

Propose a bet against the league, or against one specific person. Fill in
your name, the bet, and what's riding on it (stakes default to "a
McGillicuddy's" if you leave it blank). If you name someone in "Against," the
bet still starts OPEN until that person actually taps Accept. Nobody's on
the hook until they agree to it.

- **OPEN** bets can be accepted by anyone (tap Accept, type your name) or
  voided by whoever's involved.
- **ACCEPTED** bets can be settled once there's a winner (tap Settle, pick
  who won) or voided.
- **SETTLED** bets show the winner and stay on the board.
- A running tab at the bottom of the section counts settled wins per person.

## The passcode

The first time you post a note or a bet, or accept/settle/void one, the site
asks for the league passcode. It's the same word the regulars already know.
Ask around the bar if you don't have it. Once you enter it correctly, your
phone remembers it (and your name) so you won't be asked again on that
device. If you ever get "Wrong passcode," it'll ask again next time.

Nothing you type is stored anywhere except your own phone's browser. Nobody
else can see your passcode, and it never appears anywhere on the page.
